How to Play

🎮 Controls

Desktop: Arrow Keys or WASD to move left/right
Mobile: Use on-screen buttons or swipe

🎯 Objective

Dodge obstacles and collect power-ups to score points
Game speed increases over time - how long can you survive?

⚡ Power-ups

SPEED - Move faster for 3 seconds
SHIELD - Become invincible for 5 seconds
2X - Double score multiplier for 10 seconds
MAGNET - Auto-collect nearby coins for 8 seconds
SLOW-MO - Reduce game speed by 50% for 6 seconds

🪙 Coins & Shop

Collect golden coins scattered on the track to earn currency
Use coins to purchase new player skins in the shop (press S)
Each skin has unique visual effects and colors

🎛️ Shortcuts

P: Pause/Resume game
R: Restart game
H: Return to main menu
S: Open shop
O: Open settings
T: Show tutorial
ESC: Pause/Resume game
